JavaScript Canvas Generative Art

A portrait painted by code — and yours next

Digital Painting is a small experiment that lives entirely in your browser. It looks at a photo, decides where the picture is most wrong, and lays down one brush stroke at a time — tens of thousands of them — until a painting appears on the panel in front of you.

No image files, no libraries, no server. Only the strokes the code chose, in the order it chose them, drawn with a brush that has hairs, a paint load and a little bit of drag.

How it works

Step 1

Choose a photo

A portrait works best. JPG, PNG or WebP, read in the tab and never sent anywhere.

Step 2

Watch it paint

Seven brushes, biggest first. Pause, replay, slow it down, or finish it in one tap.

Step 3

Keep the painting

Save the finished piece as a PNG, exactly as it looks on the panel.
